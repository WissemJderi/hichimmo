# Dahech Immo

The commercial language of a real-estate lead-gen site for one agent in Sousse, Tunisia. The site exists to produce leads; everything else serves that.

## Language

**Lead**:
A person who contacts the agent through the site. The site's only conversion.
_Avoid_: client, user, customer

**Buyer/renter lead**:
A lead who inquires about a specific listing, almost always through a prefilled WhatsApp message.

**Seller/landlord lead**:
A lead who asks for an estimation of their own property. Higher value, lower volume.

**Estimation**:
The free valuation of an owner's property that serves as the hook for seller/landlord leads.
_Avoid_: appraisal, estimate, diagnostic

**Listing**:
A property advertised on the site, with photos, price, ref, and specs.
_Avoid_: product, item, ad

**Funnel**:
The path from a visitor arriving on the site to a lead existing. There are two: the listing funnel and the estimation funnel.
_Avoid_: pipeline, journey
